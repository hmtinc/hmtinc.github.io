What is AMC Replay?

AMC Replay is a powerful browser extension that analyzes your AMC movie history and creates stunning visual dashboards showing:

  • 📊 Total movies watched and money saved
  • 📅 Monthly breakdowns with detailed analytics
  • 🎯 A-List subscription value analysis
  • 📈 Beautiful charts and statistics
  • 📄 CSV export for your data
  • 📸 PNG export for sharing

Perfect for movie enthusiasts who want to track their cinema journey!

How It Works

Important: AMC Replay only works on the AMC order history page:

https://www.amctheatres.com/my-amc/history

⚠️ The script will only appear when you're logged into your AMC account and viewing your purchase history.

Once installed, you'll see a "🎬 AMC Replay" button in the AMC website header. Click it to:

  1. Automatically scan all your movie history
  2. Process and analyze your data
  3. Generate a beautiful dashboard
  4. Export your data as needed

Requirements

To use AMC Replay, you need:

  • Google Chrome Browser
  • Tampermonkey Extension
  • AMC A-List Account (with purchase history)

Installation Steps

  1. Install Chrome & Tampermonkey (links above)
  2. Click the install button below
  3. Tampermonkey will open - click "Install"
  4. Go to AMC history page and log in
  5. Look for the "🎬 AMC Replay" button in the header
  6. Click and enjoy!
💡 Tip: Make sure you're logged into your AMC account and have some movie history before using the script.

  • ✅ A-List savings calculations
  • ✅ Beautiful glassmorphism UI design
  • ✅ Export to CSV and PNG formats
  • ✅ Multi-year data support
  • ✅ Real-time processing with progress indicators
